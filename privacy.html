Privacy and terms

Short version: this site collects nothing about you. Here are the details.

Privacy

There are no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts on this site. Fonts, icons and 3D code are served from this site itself, so visiting doesn't contact any other service.

Found secrets, game progress and the name you type on the certificate are saved in your own browser's local storage. They are never sent anywhere. You can delete them with "Reset progress" in the secrets panel, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver and protect it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email me, I use your message only to reply to you. Questions about any of this: erimsaholut@icloud.com.
